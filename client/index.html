GLOBAL THERMONUCLEAR STRATEGY SIMULATION
HELP.TXT
  • Left-click / drag: select units. Double-click: select all of that type on screen.
  • Right-click: move / attack / capture / harvest. Right-drag or arrow keys: scroll. Wheel: zoom.
  • Deploy your MCV (select it, press D or right-click it) to get a Construction Yard.
  • NATO Riflemen and AT teams press D to dig into sandbags (stronger, can't move). Pact conscripts are cheap: build lots.
  • Garrisons: right-click a civilian building with basic infantry to occupy it. They fire from the windows with extra range and are safe until the building falls. Select it and press D to evacuate. Snipers and artillery clear garrisons; tanks knock the building down.
  • Aircraft: build an Air Base, then use the AIR tab. Jets have limited ammo, fly back to their pad to rearm (4 pads per base). Helicopters hover and never rearm. Only anti-air (Stinger/Strela teams, AA vehicles, SAM sites) hits aircraft hard.
  • Bridges: Ctrl + right-click a bridge to blow it up with tanks/artillery. Send an engineer into the small repair hut by the bridge to rebuild it.
  • Paradrops: capture the neutral Airport on the map with an engineer. Every few minutes the PARADROP button lights up: click it, then click anywhere on the map to drop infantry there.
  • Build from the sidebar. Right-click a sidebar item to cancel. Shift-click queues 5.
  • A+click: attack-move. S: stop. Ctrl+1-9: assign group, 1-9: select group. H: home base.
  • Select a Barracks/War Factory and right-click the ground to set a rally point.
  • Engineers capture Oil Derricks for extra income. Enter: chat. M: mute. Hover a unit to see its name; hold Alt to label every unit.
